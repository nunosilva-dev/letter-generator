## Downloads and reproduction

[Download the standalone Maven example project](/downloads/letter-generator-examples.zip) for the four quickstarts and full energy example. The advanced gallery PDFs above demonstrate additional feature combinations; their detailed authoring instructions are in the linked feature guides and the source checkout integration tests.

Maintainers regenerate previews using `./mvnw clean verify` from the library root, followed by the [website preparation/build commands](/support/documentation-build/). Gallery outputs are generated artifacts rather than hand-edited illustrations.
